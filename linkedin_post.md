Excited to announce the release of LogicFlow Version 2.0!

After a period of continuous development and improvements, I am very happy to share the second, completely revamped version of my logic circuit drawing software, LogicFlow v2.0. The application has been entirely rebuilt and redesigned to offer a professional and modern UX/UI experience, based on the feedback from the first version.

Key differences and updates (Version 1 vs Version 2):

- Modern UI: A complete transition from the traditional tkinter interface in the first version to a sleek, modern, and eye-friendly customtkinter interface.
- Calculator-style Keypad: You no longer need to type equations manually! We added a smart keypad featuring variables (A-F) and logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, XOR, etc.) so you can build your logic equations with a simple click.
- Live Step-by-Step Preview: The logic circuit is now drawn and updated in real-time as you type or press the buttons. It includes a smart system that ignores temporary syntax errors while typing to ensure a smooth experience.
- Smart Input & Processing: Full support for Copy/Paste functionality for long and complex multi-line equations, alongside automatic case-insensitivity recognition for logic gates.
- Quick Export: A convenient feature to save and export your circuit diagram as a high-resolution PNG image for easy sharing.

Programming & Design by: Sima for Digital Solutions - Ahmed Elbadawi

The software is now available as a standalone executable (.exe) file, ready for direct download and immediate use without the need for any pre-installation or setup environments.
